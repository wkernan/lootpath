# Lootpath Item Roadmap: UX brief (draft 12, 2026-09-11)

Draft 1 went through a devil's-advocate review that returned BLOCKING on three points (this week's vault drawn on next Tuesday; step counts that are a score; no verdict age anywhere). Draft 2 resolved them and the review returned MAJOR-ONLY with six majors, two introduced by the revision itself. Draft 3 resolved those; round three found two majors on the rebuilt page (a verb that looped back to its own row; Roads ordering Top Gear and Upgrade Finder verdicts in one list) and ten minors. Draft 4 resolved those; round four found one major (Upgrade Finder rows priced Mythic+ drops at QE Live's +10 level, 311, when the client previews 305 at the owner's key, against the merged 545 join) and seven minors. Draft 5 resolved those; round five found one major (a "more as a patron" tail that promised a cap lift the companion's headless fork cannot deliver, in QE Live's author's name, before the 528 conversation) and six minors. Draft 6 resolved those and round six returned CLEAR with six minors, all about attribution wording. **The owner then decided (2026-09-11): player-facing text names no source at all, never says "his", "he", "QE Live" or "the addon has determined"; it shows the rating and tells the player what to do.** Draft 7 applies that; the attribution minors are moot. Changes are marked **[R n]** through **[R5 n]** and **[O]** for the owner's decision.

## What this is

The design brief for the next Lootpath feature: surfaces that answer "how do I get a better item" and "what do I do next" inside the game, using only QE Live's numbers and the client's facts. The owner chose three: **In place** (tooltip lines and a bag glow), **Roads** (every way to an item, as steps ending in an action), **This Week** (roads on the reset calendar). Draft 2 kept all three but changed their shape and order: In place first, Roads as the expanded row under an Upgrade Map slot rather than a new panel **[R 8]**, This Week last and only when the player has named a night **[R 9]**. Draft 3 tightens the gate on This Week to "a night is named, full stop" **[R2 4]** and makes the Ladder board the Roads design as built **[R2 6]**.

## Who it is for

**Newish player** (first or second season at endgame). Knows what a dungeon is and that item level goes up. Does not reliably know what a track is, why a 305 can beat a 308, what a crest or the Catalyst does, that this week's vault is chosen before reset, that crafted gear needs sparks, that vendors sell gear for accolades. Wants to be told what to do tonight in the game's words. Fear: wasting a weekly resource (the vault pick, a Catalyst charge, crests).

**Veteran** (knows every system, ElvUI or a full suite, Pawn or a Raidbots habit). Wants density, speed and trust: the number, the source, one click. Hates tooltip clutter, tours, addons that explain the game, and any number they cannot trace. Fear: an addon confidently wrong half a tier in.

Both share the need the game does not meet: at a season start there are eight ways to get gear and no screen says which is worth the evening.

## What the research says

1. **Blizzard is absorbing tools into the base UI and says so.** Midnight added damage meters, boss alerts, the Cooldown Manager and a Journeys hub; combat addons were restricted; the stated philosophy is "You shouldn't need an add-on or an external guide to tell you these things when the game should." An addon that reads like a missing page of the game's UI swims with that; a custom world swims against it. Blizzard could build a gearing hub, and Journeys is where it would go. Lootpath's ground Blizzard will not take: the healer verdict, and the join to the player's own inventory.
2. **Journeys is the house style for progress.** Renown tracks as status bars, sections per system, a Great Vault shortcut, "current season" framing. Players expect progress as a bar with the next reward at the end.
3. **Season 2 gearing is wide.** Tracks Adventurer 282 / Veteran 295 / Champion 308 / Hero 321 / Myth 334. Crests per track; an upgrade vendor in Silvermoon whose preview window shows cost and resulting level. Sources: raids, dungeons by key level, Bountiful delves, crafting with sparks, Field Accolade vendors, zone quartermaster quests, Ritual Sites, PvP, world bosses. Icy Veins: at a season start "it can be difficult to keep track of which activities offer the best rewards." That sentence is the feature.
4. **Pawn is the incumbent mental model and its failure mode is the lesson.** Green arrows in bags and on tooltips are what players expect. Its documented weakness: right for half a tier, then wrong as trinkets and procs arrive, and players learn to distrust it. Its documented cost: bag-scan performance. Lessons: a mark must say who rated it and by how much, and must fire only when it carries information; never scan per frame.
5. **QE Live's presentation is the number source.** Percentages, ordered lists, gold for better and burnt orange for worse. Lootpath carries them unchanged.
6. **Currency and system overload is a live complaint.** A roadmap listing eight roads adds to the noise unless it collapses to a few lines by default.

## Principles

1. **It looks like a page of the game.** Blizzard's 12.x visual language, the portrait frame and tabs Lootpath has, atlases where the client has them, the game's own strings, quality colours, item icons with the level in the corner. No custom skin.
2. **Every number is someone's, and every verdict has a time.** QE Live's percent in his colours, labelled; the client's counts as the client says them. Every surface that shows a verdict shows its age as elapsed time with the date behind it, "rated 1d 2h ago (Wed 22:48)", never a bare weekday that reads as yesterday after a week **[R2 7]**. Lootpath adds no number of its own: **no step counts, no rings, no combined ordering** **[R 2]**. "No combined ordering" includes across his documents **[R3 2]**: a Top Gear verdict ("in your best set", "1.73% behind") and an Upgrade Finder percent (against what you wear) are two scales, so Roads never sorts them into one list. Rows are grouped by document, each group in his order, the groups in a fixed order that is not a ranking.
3. **Three honesty phrases, fixed, each meaning one thing** **[R 3]**:
   - **not rated**: the item has no rating. Two tails, because they have two cures **[R2 1, R3 3, O]**: "not rated · new since the last refresh" (the item did not exist at the last run; cure: the refresh loop) and "not rated · beyond the rating's item limit" (the companion offered it, but QE Live's item limit was reached once the equipped items, the vault options and the Catalyst clones took their places, as 558 built it; the item is in the leftover list the verdict file carries; cure: the owner's cap decision on 558, never a plain refresh). **No surface mentions a patron tier** **[R5 1]**: whether a patron login lifts the limit in the companion's headless run is a question for 528, and until it is answered the addon does not suggest a purchase in QE Live's name.
   - **not in your best set**: rated, and at or below what you wear (not in the best set, or 0% at the level it arrives) **[R4 1, O]**.
   - **no rating**: no run covers this road (a source that cannot be rated yet, or a plan not run).
   A line never says "not rated" without one of these tails; the phrase table on the canvas is the source of the exact strings.
4. **A comparison always names what it is compared to, and a verdict that depends on an upgrade names the level** **[R 6, R2 2]**. "1.73% behind" is never alone, and its referent is placed for a reader who does not know the vault: "1.73% behind taking the vault weapon instead" **[R4 5]**. "In your best set" carries the level QE Live assumed when that level is above what the item arrives at: "in your best set at 321". Plan names on screen are plain **[O]**: as offered, catalyzed, this week's plan, everything upgraded (internally `asOffered`, `catalyzed`, `thisWeek`, `maxed`); the glow and the tooltip follow the plan the Vault tab highlights (`thisWeek` by default, 548) **[R2 8]**.
5. **This week's vault is open now, not on Tuesday, and no deadline the client has not stated** **[R 1, R2 3]**. The vault road reads "open now · reset in 5d 11h": the timer is the client's fact; whether the offer survives the reset is not something any capture confirms, so "before reset", "last day" and "before Tuesday" are never written. Next reset's vault is "new vault: not rated until you refresh", and no verdict is ever drawn on a day after the reset.
6. **A weekly resource says what else wants it, by name, on both sides** **[R 4, R2 5]**. Counts read "1 held, 8 max". Any road that spends a resource with one held names the other road that wants it, and that road names it back: the Catalyst road says "the same charge as the vault Spaulders road", the vault Spaulders road says "the same charge as the Catalyst road: one of these, not both". A reference is never to a road that is not on the same screen.
7. **Crest steps say the cost or say it is not read, and the badge carries the condition** **[R 5, R2 2]**. The assumed level is a fact of the rating and stays ("rated at 321"). The player's ability to get there is the vendor's preview, which no capture reads yet; until it does, the step reads "crest type and cost not read · you hold 39 Hero", never a promise or a deadline, and the badge that depends on it reads "in your best set at 321" on every surface, the tooltip included.
8. **Steps are client facts, inferred, not asked** **[R 8]**. Anything the client can confirm is ticked by the client (you own the item, you hold a charge, you earned the row). Only the unknowable is the player's tick (you won the drop). A road's footer names the next undone step, never a count.
9. **Verbs from one table, and only where they go somewhere; every row ends with the thing to do** **[O]** **[R2 10, R3 1]**. The whole set: **Show item** (opens the item's Roads row; valid only from the tooltip and the vault cell, never inside a Roads row), **Show run** (the Upgrade Map by run, at that run), **Show in vault** (the Vault tab, that cell), **Options** (the settings page), **Refresh** (see 15). A road with no destination the addon may open (the Catalyst, a crafting order, a vendor, a delve) carries its next step in words and no button; the client offers no call for those and a dead button is worse than none. Never a verb outside the table.
10. **Progressive disclosure, three depths, same words.** The tooltip block is at most three roads, one sub-header and "Why this?"; Explain changes nothing on the tooltip **[R4 3, O]**. The slot's expanded row says everything about one item. This Week says everything about seven days. One road model renders all three.
11. **Newish and veteran are one screen with one switch.** Default dense. "Explain" adds one plain sentence under the first visible use of a system word in the expanded slot **[R4 8]** (track, crest, Catalyst, Bountiful, spark, plan) in the note colour; each sentence states only what the client says or what the rating names, never a cadence or a promise ("Catalyst: converts one piece into your tier set and spends one charge; the client says 1 held, 8 max"; "plan: which assumptions a rating was made under: as offered, catalyzed, this week's plan, everything upgraded") **[R 11, R3 6, R3 7, O]**. On the tooltip, "Why this?" is always the last line and Explain adds nothing there **[R3 8, O]**. No tours, no modals.
12. **The glow means one thing** **[R 7, R3 4]**: QE Live rated this item, or something it can become, as in your best set or a positive percent, under the highlighted plan. A rated-but-worse item does not glow. A not-rated item does not glow and its tooltip carries the phrase with its tail. The tooltip header carries the age as elapsed time.
13. **Cheap in combat and in bags.** Nothing in combat. Tooltip and glow read a cache keyed by item key, rebuilt on bag, vault and verdict events, never per frame.
14. **Colour is never the only signal.** Source colours ride with a word or icon; better and worse carry the sign and the percent.
15. **Refresh says what it does** **[R2 1]**. It is not one click: it runs the captures and reloads, the companion on the PC runs QE Live, and a second reload reads the answer. The verb is labelled "Refresh", its tooltip reads "runs the captures and reloads; reload again when the companion window says wrote", and the status strip shows the companion's last state (559). No surface promises that Refresh cures "beyond the rating's item limit".
16. **The plan comes first, in one or two short sentences, in the words a guildmate would type in chat** **[O2, O3]**. The owner read the finished mockups and could not tell whether to take the weapon or catalyze the shoulders; the roads were all there and the answer was not. Every surface that shows more than one road opens with the plan for this week, said the way a player says it: "Grab the staff from the vault and put your Hero crests into it. Catalyst the Lynx shoulders in your bag. Skip the vault shoulders." What to take, what each weekly resource goes on, what to leave, in that order, and nothing a road below does not also say. **The voice test is: would a guildmate type this in Discord?** So: the game's slang as players use it (catalyst as a verb, "crest it", "skip", "grab", "your bag"), items called what players call them ("the staff", "the Lynx shoulders", not the full item name with its level), contractions fine, no labels or colons, no "in your best set", no "rated", no percentages in the sentence. Those live in the roads below. The sentence is built from a small set of phrasings per verb, written by hand and tested against real weeks, never assembled from field names. On the Vault tab it is the headline. On the Upgrade Map a slot's header carries the slot's line ("Catalyst your Lynx shoulders, skip the vault ones, no crests here"). On the tooltip the first line is the item's part ("Catalyst this one"; "Grab this from the vault and crest it"; "Skip this one, the plan uses your Lynx shoulders"). If the best set spends a resource twice, the sentence says which one goes with the pick and the footnote says the other: "Catalyst the Lynx shoulders (the plan would do the chest too, but you've only got one charge)." When there is no plan the sentence says so the same way: "Nothing this week beats what you've got on." **[R3a]** The imperative the bound produces is bounded the other way too: a road whose own rating puts it behind what you wear has no next step, so it carries no "do:" at all. Where the plan for that slot is to keep what you wear, the row ends with the sentence's own clause, "keep what you've got on"; where the plan picks another road in the slot, the row ends after its facts, because an imperative that is a choice between roads is exactly what this principle forbids. The test is principle 12's - in your best set, or a positive percent - so the glow and the imperative are one decision. (The owner read "do: equip it" on a row rated 0.96% behind his own helm, 2026-09-14.) **[R3b]** And the position the plan takes on a piece you hold is about the piece, not about its key: when a held item's ITEM ID is the plan's own pick for that slot - the vault reward claimed and crested since the last refresh, or the tier piece a Catalyst pick has become - the sentence says so ("This is the vault Worldroot the plan wanted. Refresh to rate it at 315.") instead of telling the player to skip the thing the plan sent him for. The claimed copy carries its own bonus IDs and so its own key, which no document mentions, so the honesty phrase under the sentence still reads "not rated - new since the last refresh" and no rating is invented. The vault road for that slot reads "claimed - in your bags" rather than "open now" and its verb becomes Refresh; nothing here reads the vault, it reads the bags. A piece you are WEARING is never "arrived": the slot already has its own road for what is on the character. (The owner took the Worldroot, crested it to 315, and the plan told him to skip it, 2026-09-14 night.) The other way round, a plan that is behind the bags says what cures that: where a slot holds anything the rating never saw, the tooltip's header and the Upgrade Map's slot header both end "/lootpath refresh".


## The surfaces, in build order

**1. In place.** GameTooltip gets a block headed "Lootpath · <slot> · rated 1d 2h ago" **[R3 11, O]**: the hovered item's part of the plan as the first line, in chat voice ("Catalyst this one"; "Grab this from the vault and crest it"; "Skip this one, the plan uses your Lynx shoulders") **[O2, O3]**, then the item's own road, then, under "other roads for this slot", the top row of each remaining group in his order until three roads are shown **[R4 2]**, one line each: source tag, the item as it would arrive, his badge with its referent and level, the player's count if a resource is involved. Bag slot and vault cell glow only under principle 12. The last line is always "Why this?"; the honesty phrase with its tail replaces the item's road when nothing is rated. This is the surface Blizzard will never build and the one most players will use most.

**Surface 1, amended 2026-09-14 after the owner's first real screens (R-2a, WKE-571; ARCHITECTURE.md §7).** Four rules were added or tightened, and none of them changes surface 2:
- **Every piece you HOLD opens on a sentence, rated or not** - bags, worn, bank. The plan takes a position on everything you own: use it, catalyst it, or skip it. A road to something you do NOT have (a journal drop, a crafted row, a delve row) still has none, which is the boundary R-2 drew and it stands.
- **"Other roads" are RATED roads only**, best-set or positive percent first. Principle 10's three is a cap, not a quota, and a "no rating" line under a best-set pick spends one of three on nothing.
- **No clause whose referent is on another screen.** The crest COST clause (principle 7's vendor-window gate) and the "same charge as" rival clause (principle 6's own rule) belong to the row, where the crest counts and the rival row are visible. The Upgrade Map keeps both. A road whose item the client has not named yet says what kind of thing it is (`a crafted piece (331)`), never its item ID.
- **The last line names its destination**: `Why this? · /lootpath map`. Principles 10 and 11 keep it always-last; principle 9 requires that it go somewhere, and on a tooltip it cannot be clicked.

**2. Roads, as the slot's row.** On the Upgrade Map by slot, the slot header carries the slot's plan sentence **[O2]**, and the section expands to its roads in three groups, in a fixed order that is not a ranking **[R3 2]**: **Your best set** (the set rating under the highlighted plan: the pick for the slot as the first row, then the rated alternatives, each with its "behind" referent), **Other rated sources** (per-item ratings in rated order, with the scale named once in the group header: "percents are against what you wear · at your key's preview level, per the client") **[R3 10, R4 1, O]**, and **No rating** (dim). A Mythic+ row is valued the way the Upgrade Map already values every journal drop since 545: the item level is the client's preview for the player's key, and the percent comes from whichever of the companion's key-level documents carries that level; QE Live's +10 document (311) is used only when the client previews 311. A row's figures all come from that one document; if the upgraded or cap figure has to come from another key level, the row names it **[R5 7]**. The upgraded and cap levels on a row are his, read from the export, and labelled "upgraded 321 at" and "at its cap 334" **[R4 4, O]**. A row for what the player wears, tagged **Keep (what you wear)**, is part of the tag set and appears in QE Live's set group whenever a "behind" on that screen refers to it **[R4 7]**. Each row: source tag, the item, badge with referent and level, the next undone step in words, and a verb only when one goes somewhere. The same row opens from the tooltip's "Why this?". No new panel, no ticks to click except the unknowable. Delves and Crafted rows come from the Upgrade Finder document the companion already writes; vendor gear is "no rating" until a season table exists.

**3. This Week, only when a night is named** **[R2 4]**. Shown only when the player has named at least one night in Options; otherwise the tab is a single line, "name a key or raid night in Options and This Week fills in", with the verb. Seven columns from today, reset marked. A named night holds one chip: the run whose best number from QE Live is highest for that night (542's by-run arrangement, which is Lootpath's ordering of QE Live's numbers and adds none), nothing else, so a day has one scale **[R5 2]**. The open vault and the charge are not on this surface; they live on the tooltip and the Roads row. The one vault chip is on the reset day: "new vault: not rated until you refresh" **[R3 5, O]**. Days without a night are blank; the header carries the reset timer and one Options verb.

## Copy voice

**[O] No source is named on any surface.** Player-facing text never says "QE Live", "his", "he", or "the addon has determined". The screen shows the rating and says what to do: "in your best set", "+3.08%", "do: take it". Headers carry "rated 1d 2h ago" for the age. The four phrases are **not rated · new since the last refresh**, **not rated · beyond the rating's item limit**, **not in your best set**, **no rating**. Scenario names are plain: as offered, catalyzed, this week's plan, everything upgraded. The engine is not named anywhere in the addon's UI, Options included, by the owner's decision; the trace from a rating to its source lives in the repository (the brain, the companion's verdict file) and nowhere on screen. The research lesson that "a mark must say who rated it" is consciously set aside here; what the mark still says is its age and its plan. "Beyond the item limit" reads as the rating's limit, and the Roads row explains it in one line without naming whose limit it is. The product rule is unchanged: the addon computes nothing, and the screen never claims it did; "rated" is true whichever engine writes the verdict file.

Second person, present tense, the game's nouns. "Take Scavenger's Spaulders (308) from the vault · open now · reset in 5d 11h." Every rated road's row ends with "do: ..." naming the thing to do; a "no rating" row and a "not read" step carry their words without "do:", because the addon does not send a player to work an unrated road **[R8 2, R8 5]**. The imperative line carries its condition when the badge has one ("do: take it · rated at 321, crests not readable") and never names two roads ("do: take one vault reward", with the two options listed in the badge text) **[R8 3, R8 4]**. **[O] This retires the old "never you should" rule: the owner wants the addon to tell the player what to do.** What keeps it honest is what the imperative is allowed to be: the road's own next step ("do: take it", "do: run the key"), never a comparison between roads and never a choice made for the player; when two roads compete for one resource the row says "one of these, not both" and lists both. Never a deadline. Numbers stay numbers. Struck: "or higher", "win the roll", "before reset", "last day", "best rated run tonight" without a run, a bare "+10", "collect at 331", "the vendor in Silvermoon", and any delve step until a delve capture exists (the row reads "not read", without "do:").

## What we will not do

No score of ours, not even a step count or a combined ordering. No glow that means anything but "rated, in your set or better". No automatic equipping or prompts. No custom skin. No tour. No combat behaviour. No per-frame scanning. No verdict drawn past the reset.

## Dropped from the concept set

Orbit (rings are a score), Lines (stations are a count, and lines look equal), the flow chart and the four-column roads panel (their lanes and columns survive as the Roads groups). The canvas now shows only the chosen design; their reasoning is this section **[R4 6]**.

## Buildability (engineering pass, 2026-09-11, read against Blizzard's 12.1 annotations under `.luals/` and the installed Baganator, ElvUI and Pawn sources)

**Every surface is buildable. Three things are hard, one thing is not readable, and one standing decision must be reopened first.**

- **Reopen §7 (2026-09-05, "tooltips descoped") with a dated entry** before any issue is filed: surface 1 reverses it.
- **Tooltip block: feasible with caveats; the hook is measured before it is used (R-0, WKE-561).** Blizzard calls a post-call as `func(tooltip, tooltipData)` and declares **no remover** for it (`TooltipDataHandler.lua:199, 298`), so anything registered is registered for the session; `TooltipUtil.GetDisplayedItem` returns `name, hyperlink, tooltipData.id`, and the third value is real in the shipped source even though the annotated `TooltipData` class does not declare it - key off the hyperlink because the id is undeclared, not because it is absent. `TooltipDataProcessor.AddTooltipPostCall(Enum.TooltipDataType.Item, fn)` plus `TooltipUtil.GetDisplayedItem(tooltip)` gives the hyperlink for bag items (Blizzard, Baganator and ElvUI bags all route hovers through `SetBagItem`) and journal items (`SetHyperlink`). The callback fires for every item tooltip in the game, chat links and merchants included, so it must be an O(1) cache lookup keyed by `ns.ItemKey` or an immediate return. Key off the hyperlink only, for the reason above. Pawn is installed and will put its own block on the same tooltips; Lootpath's block sits below it.
- **Bag glow: feasible, three code paths, Pawn contention on two.** Blizzard bags: `hooksecurefunc(ContainerFrameMixin, "UpdateItems")` and iterate the valid item buttons, drawing on Lootpath's own texture, never on the `UpgradeIcon` Pawn owns. Baganator: the public `Baganator.API.RegisterUpgradePlugin` or `RegisterCornerWidget`; only one upgrade plugin is active at a time, so choosing Lootpath's displaces Pawn's arrow. ElvUI bags: the slot's arrow is Pawn-only and a Lootpath glow means hooking `UpdateSlot` on a module that changes every release. **Which bag addon the owner actually has enabled is not recorded anywhere; find out before building.** **R-0 (2026-09-12) halved this:** his own `env` transcript of 2026-09-09 08:59 says Baganator, Syndicator, ElvUI and Pawn are ALL loaded, so the question is which bag FRAME is live rather than which addon is installed - `capture env` now records that line every time, and the owner's spike run answers the rest.
- **The vault cell is Lootpath's own** (`VaultPanel.lua` builds the hover), so the block appends there directly; Blizzard's Great Vault window never shows an item tooltip for a preview and is out of scope.
- **Roads as the slot's row: feasible on what exists.** `UpgradeMapPanel.Elements` already flattens sections into typed elements over a `WowScrollBoxList` with per-character collapse; two new element kinds follow the pattern. Every input exists: `QEImport` scenarios and alternatives, `UFImport.LookupAcrossLevels` with drop types, the journal's names, `Currencies.Read`, `Companion.Excluded`, the export's time.
- **This Week: feasible.** Reset from `C_DateAndTime.GetSecondsUntilWeeklyReset`, weekday from `GetCurrentCalendarTime`; nights through `Settings.RegisterProxySetting` on the existing Options category; the run chip is 542's by-run ordering, which the brief names as Lootpath's arrangement.
- **Crest cost: not readable for a vault option or a drop, ever.** `C_ItemUpgrade.GetItemUpgradeItemInfo` answers only for an owned item placed in the open vendor window, which is an action, not a read. "Crest type and cost not read" is therefore the permanent state for vault and drop rows; for an owned item it stays "not read" until the owner decides whether a vendor-window capture is allowed (a second journal-style exception).
- **Keystone level: feasible** (`C_MythicPlus.GetOwnedKeystoneLevel`, `GetOwnedKeystoneChallengeMapID`, `C_ChallengeMode.GetMapUIInfo`), so "your key +8 · rated at the client's preview level" can be real on day one.
- **Delves: the key is a currency whose ID no capture has read; which delves are Bountiful is not exposed by any `C_DelvesUI` function.** "do: not read" stands. **Built since R-3 and R-4 (2026-09-13/14):** the 33 `Delves` rows every Upgrade Finder document carries are a road under each slot (R-3 draws them under `Other rated sources`; the road says `Delves · not read`) and, since R-4 (WKE-565), a `Delves` card in the by-run view saying `Delves - key and Bountiful state not read`. Neither asserts anything about WHICH delve: "Bountiful" is not something a committed file or a client call confirms about these rows, so it names what is unread instead.
- **Crafting: the spark count is feasible once its item ID is captured; materials and orders need the crafter's open profession or order window and cannot be read passively.** Spark count only. **Built since R-3 and R-4 (2026-09-13/14):** the 18 `Crafted` rows are a road under each slot and, since R-4 (WKE-565), a `Crafting` card by run. The crafted road says `the rating assumes Crit / Haste` beside `spark and materials not read`, and the card's rows read `Crafted, Crit / Haste - spark and materials not read`: the stats line is the export's own `settings.craftedStats`, while `settings.craftedLevel` is an INDEX (2 while the rows are 331) and is never shown. These rows carry no key level, because the five key-level documents of one run value them identically and only the content type moves them (ARCHITECTURE.md §4, §9).
- **Vendor gear: not readable from the client.** Needs a season table as a Data file, then the companion. Stays illustrative.
- **The excluded list carries the item ID and the bonus IDs since C-10 (WKE-567, 2026-09-12)**; "beyond the item limit" is an `ns.ItemKey` comparison, asked through `ns.Companion.IsExcluded(excluded, key, item)`, which answers with the entry and with HOW it matched (`key`, or `name+level`). A verdict file written before C-10 carries name, slot and level alone and is still matched by name and level, so a road built on this must expect both answers - and the name+level one is only as good as the names are unique. A Catalyst clone also carries `originalItem`, the item it was made from, because the clone's own ID is a tier piece the character does not own.
- **"Behind X" referent: read, and now checked (R-0, WKE-561, 2026-09-12).** Over the committed `thisWeek` Dungeon document `spec/fixtures/qe/qe-droptimizer-Hotornot-hdaldwpeakpb.json`, parsed by `ns.QEImport.Parse` in `spec/qeimport_spec.lua`: differential **7** (`scorePercent 1.729166724018797`, `hpsDifference -6009`) lists exactly two items - the vault **Shoulder 271526 at 321** and the **2H Weapon 251935 at 308** - and the top set holds that same weapon at **321** with `isVault` true. **The referent is read from the differential's own item list**: the swap is one item ID seen at two levels, so "1.73% behind taking the vault weapon" is a read and never an inference. **"Hero track" on a vault item is not something any `C_Item` call returns**; it is a tooltip line or omitted, so player copy does not say it.

- **Cache:** one map by item key, one by item ID and level (the Upgrade Finder key), and one by slot for "other roads", built once per verdict and rebuilt on `BAG_UPDATE_DELAYED`, `PLAYER_EQUIPMENT_CHANGED`, `WEEKLY_REWARDS_UPDATE`, `CURRENCY_DISPLAY_UPDATE`, item-data load results and companion import, debounced. **What R-1 learned while building the model (2026-09-12, WKE-562; every figure in ARCHITECTURE.md §9).**

- **The referent really is readable, and it is the other slots the alternative names.** Differential 7 of the `thisWeek` Dungeon document lists the vault Spaulders AND the 2H Weapon, so the shoulder road's "1.73% behind" reads *taking the vault weapon instead* and the weapon's Keep road reads the same percentage *taking the catalyzed shoulders instead*. Both are read off his own item list; neither is inferred.
- **A row's figures must come from one run, and his own `dropType` supplies the words.** `bonus` is the "upgraded 321 at" figure and `max` is the "at its cap 334" one. Reading other levels across every key-level document instead put a "308" and a "311" on the Mythic+ row that were another run's `drop`, which is the mixing [R5 7] forbids.
- **"Put your Hero crests into it" is not buildable and is not built.** Which crest an upgrade takes is not readable (the note above), so the sentence says "and crest it". What you hold is readable and stays on the crest step, in the client's own names: "you hold 356 Adventurer Mistcrest, 2 Champion Mistcrest, ...".
- **"The staff" needs the item's type word, which no committed export or capture carries.** `ns.Roads.ShortName` prefers a `subType` when a caller has one and otherwise says the last word of the name, so the owner's own weapon reads as "the Worldroot" until a surface hands the client's word in. The possessive and "<A> of <B>" rules give "the Lynx shoulders" and "the Hide chest" exactly as the canvas has them.
- **"do: raid Heroic" only reads well when the difficulty label is one word.** The client's own label for the committed walk's 324 two-hander is "Mythic raid" (difficulty 16, not Heroic), so the imperative is "do: raid it · tick when it drops" and the difficulty stays on the row's source line where it already was.
- **The canvas's "2.74% behind" on the weapon's Keep road belongs to `everything upgraded`, not to this week's plan.** Under `this week's plan` that road is 1.73% behind. See ARCHITECTURE.md §9.
- **A vault option no set of his mentions is "not in your best set", never "not rated".** 558 read in his importer that every vault option is active from the moment it is imported, so it cannot have fallen outside the item limit: the run saw it and left it. The Preyhunter's Lantern is that row on the owner's own week.
- **The unrated half of a slot is big.** Every journal drop the export does not value at the level the client previews is a "no rating" road, which is around thirty rows for a busy slot on the 478-drop walk. The model carries them and counts them; R-2 and R-3 decide what a tooltip and a row show of them.

**What R-3 learned while building surface 2 (2026-09-12, WKE-564; every figure in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §9).**

- **The canvas's group order for "Other rated sources" is not reproducible and is not built.** The issue asked for "the document's order (raid, crafted, Mythic+, delves)" and the canvas draws the weapon slot in exactly that order, but no document carries it: the Upgrade Finder export is one flat item list and its own order is the percent it gave. R-1 already settled this - inside the `item` group the order is his percent, best first, with the build order behind it for stability - and the brief itself says "rated order", so the merged model's order stands and the issue's parenthetical is a description of which sources appear rather than of how they sort.
- **The slot's rows ARE its roads, and one join went with them.** M3-3's candidate list printed a Top Gear coverage sentence on a journal drop whose exact item key the top set carried ("QE Live: in your best set" on the drop row). A road for an item you own lives in the `set` group and a journal drop lives in `item` or `none`, so that sentence is no longer on a drop row. On the owner's own data this costs nothing, and `UpgradeMapPanel.lua`'s own header says why: the committed walk's keyed rows carry exactly one bonus ID (3524) where the Top Gear items carry two to seven, so the exact-key join covers **no** drop in any committed pair. The model still counts it (`counts.covered`).
- **"First visible use" is counted over the drawn list, not over the model.** The Explain sentence for "Catalyst" arrives under the slot's own plan sentence ("Catalyst your Lynx shoulders...") rather than under the Catalyst row, because that sentence is what the reader meets first. A collapsed slot explains nothing.
- **"no crests here" is not a use of "crest".** Explain matches whole words, so the plural in the slot sentence does not spend the crest sentence before the row that says what is not readable earns it.
- **The row's muted facts are the road's own steps, marked.** R-1's steps gained one flag, `fact`, separating what is KNOWN (what the client says you hold; what is not readable at all) from what there is to DO. The doing steps stay in the footer and the "do:" line; the facts ride beside the badge. `ns.Roads.Facts(road)` is that list and R-2's tooltip reads the same one.
- **The vault road's countdown is the transcript's own.** Over the committed week it reads "reset in 6d 21h"; the canvas's "5d 11h" was illustrative. `ns.Roads.ResetText` formats the client's `secondsUntilWeeklyReset` and says nothing about what the reset does.

**What R-2 learned while building surface 1 (2026-09-14, WKE-563; every figure in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §9).**

- **The cache is the tab's own model, not a second reading of it.** `Panel.Model` already calls `ns.Roads.ForSlot` for every slot it draws, so the map takes those road tables by reference and builds only the slots the model does not reach. On the owner's week that is 16 slots and 424 keys, built once per verdict; a guard counts 0 calls to the model, the walk or a bag scan across 150 hovers.
- **The plan sentence has nothing to say about a road the plan is not about.** Principle 16 gives the tooltip "the item's part of the plan", and for a piece you hold, a Catalyst clone, a vault option or what you wear that is a clause (`Catalyst this one.`, `Grab this from the vault and crest it.`, `Keep this on.`, `Skip this one, the plan uses your Lynx shoulders.`). For a journal drop, a crafted row or a delve row it is nothing: those live in the `item` group, the best set takes no position on them, and `Skip this one` on a dungeon drop reads as "skip the dungeon", which is a thing to do that no document said. Those hovers open on their road line instead.
- **The possessive is the pick's, not the reader's.** `the plan uses your Lynx shoulders` for a piece you hold or a clone of one, `the plan uses the vault Spaulders` for an option that is not yours until you take it, `the plan keeps your Periapt on` for a Keep road. One rule, read off `road.kind`.
- **"Three roads, never more" has to be counted by the tooltip.** The model has exactly three groups, so `ForItem` can never hand over more than two others and `ns.Roads.TOOLTIP_ROADS` is true by accident rather than by rule - a mutation of the constant left the suite green. The block counts its own cap now, and the guard is a handcrafted answer with four others.
- **The block belongs only on an item the addon actually knows.** The post-call fires for every item tooltip in the game. Answering a chat link or a merchant's stock with `not rated · new since the last refresh` would be a sentence about a question nobody asked, so the block is drawn only for a key the map carries: bags, the vault, the walk, the export. That is exactly the three surfaces this brief names.
- **One glow interface, one adapter per bag addon, and Pawn keeps its arrow.** The core says `ns.Glow.Wants(key)` and nothing else about bags. Baganator's `RegisterCornerWidget` is the way in - `RegisterUpgradePlugin` would displace Pawn's arrow, because only one upgrade plugin is active at a time - and Blizzard's `ContainerFrameMixin:UpdateItems` hook is the fallback, drawing Lootpath's own texture and never the `UpgradeIcon` Pawn owns. A bag window with no adapter gets no glow and every word of the tooltip.
- **The glow is the item's, not the road's.** An item is marked when ANY road into it is wanted. On the owner's own bags that is the Lynx Spaulders and the Hide of Pestilence, both Catalyst conversions the plan takes; the vault Spaulders, 1.73% behind, stay dark beside them.
- **A mark is drawn at the size it will be seen at, not borrowed from a screen that had room (R-2b, 2026-09-14, WKE-575).** R-2 gave the bag mark the Great Vault's own selection glow, `evergreen-weeklyrewards-reward-selected`, so that the bag and the Vault tab would share one picture. The owner measured that atlas on his client: **214 x 121**, a wide soft-edged banner made to sit behind a vault cell. Squeezed by `SetAllPoints` into Baganator's 15-point corner widget it is a smear, and his slot looked empty while every line of the diagnosis said `glow yes`. Two surfaces sharing a picture was a wish, not a rule, and a picture that cannot be seen shares nothing. The corner mark is now drawn by the addon itself - Blizzard's flat `WHITE8X8` in two layers, a near-black square filling the frame with the accent square inset one point inside it - so it is opaque, has its own edge over pale icon art, and depends on no atlas anyone would have to measure. The Vault tab keeps the glow at the size it was drawn for, and the accent is the same `#FFDF14` in both places, so the two surfaces still mean the same thing in the same colour.
