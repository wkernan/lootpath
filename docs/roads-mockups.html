Lootpath Roads

The one design we are building: three surfaces that answer "how do I get a better item" and "what do I do tonight" inside the game, using only the ratings the companion writes and the client's facts. Two real examples from the owner's captures and the exports of 2026-09-08 and 09-09. Every number on screen is a rating or the client's own count; no source is named on any surface; the only illustrative element is marked.

The shape

Three Surfaces, One Model

A road is one way to get an item: its source, the item as it would arrive, its rating, and the next thing the player can do. One road model renders three surfaces, so a road reads the same wherever it appears.

1 · In place, built firstHover any item in your bags, the vault or the journal and Blizzard's own tooltip gains at most three Lootpath lines: the item's own road, then other roads for that slot. The bag slot glows only when a road is "in your best set" or a positive percent. No window to open.
2 · Roads, built secondOn the Upgrade Map's by-slot view, each slot expands to its roads in three groups, never one ranking: your best set, then other rated sources, then roads with no rating. A verb only where one goes somewhere. Reached from the tooltip's "Why this?".
3 · This Week, built thirdShown only when you have named a key or raid night in Options. Each named night holds one chip: the run with the highest-rated drop for that night. Without a night it is one line and the Options verb.

Large and smallEvery badge is the rating for the item as it would arrive, against what you wear: the raid at the journal's Heroic 324, the key at the client's preview 305. The raid and the craft are the big ones, the key is the cheap one, and nothing on screen says so in any other way.
Why the pick is in its own group"In your best set" is a rating of a whole set; a per-item percent is a different question. Putting them in one ranked list would be an ordering of our own, so they are two groups with the scale named.
The gapVendor and turn-in gear needs a season table for the items and one refresh so they get rated. Until then the road is "no rating" and dim.

One scale per dayA named night holds one chip: the run with the highest-rated drop, an arrangement of ratings that adds none. The open vault and the charge are not here; they live on the tooltip and the Roads row. The one vault chip is the reset day's "new vault: not rated".
No deadlinesThe reset timer is the client's fact. Whether this week's offer survives the reset is not something any capture confirms, so nothing here says "before reset" or "last day".
Honest emptiesA day without a night is blank. Nothing says "nothing rated": the runs are rated, the player simply has no night there.
The rules every surface obeys

Words and Verbs

PhraseMeansCure
not rated · new since the last refreshthe item did not exist at the last run, so it has no rating yetthe refresh loop
not rated · beyond the rating's item limitthe run rates a fixed number of items; the equipped items, the vault options and the Catalyst clones take their places first (558) and this one was left out; it is in the leftover listthe owner's cap decision on 558; not a refresh
not in your best setrated, and at or below what you wear (not in the best set, or 0% at the level it arrives)none needed; it is an answer
no ratingno run covers this road (a source that cannot be rated yet, a plan not run)a new source table or plan, then a refresh
VerbGoes to
Show itemthe item's Roads row on the Upgrade Map; only from the tooltip ("Why this?") and the vault cell, never inside a Roads row
Show runthe Upgrade Map by run, scrolled to that run
Show in vaultthe Vault tab, that cell
Optionsthe settings page: nights, Explain, the plan
Refreshruns the captures and reloads; its tooltip says to reload again when the companion window says wrote
(none)a road whose next step is not something the addon may open: the Catalyst, a crafting order, a vendor, a delve. Words, no button.

A road is drawn from the client's facts. Its value is the rating for the item as it would arrive that way, with its referent and its level. Rows are grouped by the kind of rating and kept in rated order inside each group; the groups are not a ranking. A drop is rated at the level the client says it arrives. Every rating carries its age. No count, ring or score beyond the ratings appears anywhere. Where there is no rating, the line says which of the four phrases applies. No source is named on any surface.

  1. Build order: the road model (pure Lua over the committed captures and exports), then In place, then Roads as the slot's row, then Delves and Crafted from the export's extra rows, then This Week, then vendor gear when a season table exists.
  2. Not built: the flow chart, the four-column roads panel, the metro map, the orbit. Their counts and rings were the reason.
  3. Buildable, per the engineering pass against Blizzard's 12.1 annotations: the tooltip block (a post-call that must be an O(1) cache lookup), the bag glow (three bag implementations, Pawn on two of them; find out which bag addon is enabled first), the Roads row (two new element kinds in the existing list), This Week (reset timer, calendar weekday, nights in Options). Not readable from the client: the crest cost for a vault item or a drop, which delves are Bountiful, crafting materials. The first issue is a measurement spike on the tooltip hook, not a surface.
